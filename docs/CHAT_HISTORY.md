# Saved chats and room messages

Private chats and room messages stay in SQLite until you delete them. Restarting RustySoul, closing a chat, or leaving a room keeps the saved messages. Reopening a conversation loads its most recent messages; use **Load older messages** to continue back through its history. New arrivals appear alongside the existing conversation.

The Messages page lists saved private conversations. **Close chat** removes that conversation from the sidebar and keeps its history. The next unread conversation is selected, moving downward through the list and wrapping to the top. If none are unread, the chat immediately above is selected, or the one below when there is no chat above. Closing the last conversation leaves an empty view. Closed conversations stay hidden in that browser after refresh; enter the username or use **Message** in a user's options to reopen one. A new message brings its conversation back, including messages received while the browser was closed. These sidebar preferences are stored per Soulseek account in the browser; they do not delete or change saved messages. The Rooms page also has **Saved room history**, which lets you read previous conversations while disconnected or after leaving a room. You must join a room to send new room messages.

Rooms and private chats show the same unread badges: an overall count in the navigation, a count for each room or conversation, and a **Jump to latest** action when reading older messages. The current conversation is marked read only while the browser is focused and its latest message is visible. Read markers survive restarts. On the first upgrade to private-message notifications, existing private history is treated as read; subsequent arrivals receive unread alerts.

Click any username to open its options. **Ignore messages from this user** hides that person's messages in every room and direct chat, including saved history, without deleting existing history. Matching ignores ASCII letter case. Ignored incoming messages are discarded and do not generate unread alerts; unignoring reveals previously saved messages again. **Ban this user from my shares** separately refuses their share-list and upload requests and cancels their active uploads. Both options can be enabled together, changed without being connected, and managed in Settings → People & sharing.

Choose **Delete history** in a conversation header, choose **All messages**, **Older than a number of days**, **Before a date and time**, or **Within a date range**, then confirm with **Delete history permanently**. Dates use the browser time zone. A range includes its starting time and excludes its ending time; older-than and before also exclude the cutoff itself. Every option applies to saved history, including older pages and ignored messages. Cancel leaves everything in place. Deletion applies only to that selected private chat or room. New messages received or sent afterwards start a new saved history. Existing backup files retain the messages they contained when created; restoring an older backup can bring that history back.

History loads in small pages to keep the browser and daemon lightweight. The live event stream keeps a 500-message preview across conversations; this preview does not limit or erase the permanent database history. There is no automatic message-history expiration. Messages already purged by older releases are available only if retained in an existing backup.

Choose **Search messages** in a room or private conversation to search its full saved history. Use message text, an exact sender username, or both. Sender matching ignores ASCII case. Text is a literal substring (ASCII case-insensitive); percent signs, underscores and quotes have no wildcard meaning. Results arrive in pages of 100; **Load older matches** reaches the beginning of history. Searches work while disconnected, keep ignored users hidden, and do not acknowledge unread activity. **Back to conversation** restores the live conversation and its draft. Search again to include later arrivals. Clicking or keyboard-activating anywhere on a private-conversation row opens that chat; user details remain available from the conversation header.
